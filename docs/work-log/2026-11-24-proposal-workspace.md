## 2026-11-24 — Open a proposal workspace with a first draft when Dane pursues a job (#16)

Pressing **Pursue** on a job in the Inbox now opens a workspace: the job on one
side and a first draft proposal on the other, written by the AI. This is the
first text the app writes that Dane might send to a client, so what it may draw
on is fenced in: the AI sees the job post (treated as a stranger's words, never
as orders) and only the portfolio projects Dane has switched to "approved for
use". It is told never to invent a project or a number, and never to call a
planned project finished. A test with a pretend AI proves an unapproved project
never reaches it.

Pursue makes a draft and nothing else. Nothing is approved, sent or submitted,
and the page says so. Pressing Pursue again opens the same draft rather than
writing over it. If the AI can't write one (say it is busy), the page says why
and offers a button to try again.

The job's screening questions, when the post lists them, appear under the
draft, each flagged "Needs an answer" until Dane saves one. He can add any the
app missed. Approving a draft and checking its claims are the next two tasks.

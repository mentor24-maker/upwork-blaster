## 2026-11-24 — Show jobs as ranked cards with Pursue, Pass and Revisit (#14)

There is now an Inbox page, linked first on the home page. It shows the jobs
you have saved as cards, best fit first. The top five come first and the rest
sit below them.

Each card shows:

- the job's title and link, budget, job type, when it was posted, and its
  Connects cost
- what is known about the client
- the post's first sentence, as a short "need"
- the fit score and how sure the app is about it
- your best matching portfolio project
- the biggest thing the app did not know
- the reasons behind the score, which you can open

Anything the post did not say shows as "unknown". "Your time it would take"
also says unknown for now. Estimating that is later work, and the app does
not guess.

Each card has three buttons: Pursue, Pass and Revisit, with an optional box
for your reason. Every press is kept, so the history of what you decided is
never lost. A job you pass leaves the inbox. It stays in a folded "Passed"
list at the bottom, with a button to bring it back. Passing never changes
your preferences or any score. It only records what you decided, and a test
checks that.

Jobs that hit one of your hard exclusions, like a technology you don't take,
are listed separately with the rule they met and are never ranked. Jobs the
app could not score are listed separately too, with the reason. Pursue does
not write a draft yet. That comes with the proposal workspace.

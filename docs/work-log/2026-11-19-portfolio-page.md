## 2026-11-19 — Add a portfolio evidence page proposals can draw on (#11)

The app now has a Portfolio page. Dane can list the projects he is allowed to point to in a proposal: the project's name, whether it is delivered, in progress or only planned, what it does, and links to it. Each entry has an "approved for use" switch, and entries can be edited or removed.

Why it matters: when the app starts drafting proposals, it will only ever be able to cite entries Dane has switched to approved. Anything planned or not yet signed off stays out of a proposal, so nothing gets claimed that he has not agreed to. His real portfolio is typed into the app on the Mini, never stored in this public repository.
